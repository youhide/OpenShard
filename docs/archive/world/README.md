# Archived world documents

One document: the consolidation that **was the map area's entry point** until
the domain was given roles. It is kept for its reasoning, not for its
instructions — where it and a live document disagree, the live one is right.

| Document | What it was |
|---|---|
| [`map_rebuild.md`](map_rebuild.md) | *The map, in three layers.* Written to close the gap nine plans left between them — none of them said what a map **is** at runtime — it stated the matryoshka (ground, statics, the live layer), the invariant that a bake sees exactly what is below the live layer, and ordered the nine into three eras: **R** the map you hold, **P** the map you search, **S** the map you change. |

**What replaced it.** [`../../world/README.md`](../../world/README.md) is the
domain's one entry point and carries the status and what is open;
[`design_layers.md`](../../world/design_layers.md) carries the routing rule over
the three layers; the era records are in
[`../../world/evidence/`](../../world/evidence). The eras themselves are spent:
R is over, P has no open node, and what is left of S is
[`what_a_change_costs`](../../../plans/world/what_a_change_costs/PLAN.md).

**Why it is archived rather than rewritten.** Two documents both claimed to be
the entry point for the map, and each of the nine plans kept a third copy of the
status inside itself. This one held the best account, so its substance is what
the domain README was written from — and a consolidation that has been
consolidated is a record, which is this directory. Its own node text (R1 to R5,
era S's "a tile of ground moved", the decisions it took between plans) is the
long form of rows that are one line each in the README.

Its line-number references into other documents were dropped in the move: a
`#L308` into a file that has since been renamed and rewritten points at nothing
in particular, so the links now name the document and not the line.
